Invite-Only Beta

Cue Room's first beta is invite-only through TestFlight. Beta builds unlock Plus features at no charge for invited testers.

What To Test

  1. Open the built-in sample play.
  2. Record several cast lines.
  3. Play recordings back from the Cast tab.
  4. Run guided lines and full reads from Rehearse.
  5. Export and import a .cueroomshow file.
  6. Use Nearby Workroom sync with a second iPhone when possible.
