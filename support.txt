Cue Room Support

Contact curtaincallsupport@icloud.com for help with Cue Room. Include your app version, iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and a short description of what happened. Do not send API keys, passwords, or scripts you do not have permission to share.

Importing your script

Choose a PDF, Word DOCX, or text file, scan pages with the camera, or select photos. Pro import uses OpenAI exclusively. Tap Continue with OpenAI to send the selected pages, images, or document text using your saved key and API credits. Review the characters, their roles, dialogue, and excluded passages, then choose your part and save. Completed sections are saved on your device; after an interruption, reopen Add a script and continue. For unclear scans, choose brighter, sharper images.

Rehearsing without OpenAI

Installed Apple voices work without an OpenAI key. Choose your role, then rehearse the scene. Listening is the default: finish your line and pause for the reader to reply. Rehearsal options let you change reply timing. Next is always available if recognition misses a cue. Camera and microphone features depend on device support and permissions.

OpenAI features

OpenAI is required for Pro document import and optional for rehearsing saved scripts. Connect your own API key and enable requests in Apple & OpenAI settings. OpenAI usage is billed separately and is not included in the Cue Room subscription. Reader voices use source context for delivery and prepare one upcoming cue while you speak. Understand paraphrased cues in Rehearsal options is a separate text-sharing choice, using at most two short OpenAI checks per turn. Never send your key to support. You can turn off requests, remove your key, or clear downloaded AI audio in those settings.

Subscription and billing

Use Restore purchases with the Apple Account that subscribed. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple subscription settings (https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions). Deleting the app does not cancel billing. For a refund request, visit Apple Report a Problem (https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

Your data

Export scripts from the script menu to keep an independent copy. Read the Privacy Policy (privacy.html) and Terms of Use (terms.html) for details about local storage, sharing, optional AI processing, and billing.
