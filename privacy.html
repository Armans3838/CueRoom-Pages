Curtain Call

Privacy Policy

Effective date: May 12, 2026

Curtain Call is a local-first rehearsal app for casts working on theater productions.

Data Collection

Curtain Call does not collect personal data in the current MVP. The app does not operate a Curtain Call backend, account system, analytics service, advertising network, or tracking system.

Scripts And Recordings

Scripts, character lines, show metadata, and actor recordings are stored locally on the user's device.

Users may choose to share rehearsal data by exporting a .curtaincallshow package through iOS sharing tools such as Files, AirDrop, Messages, or Mail, or by joining a nearby workroom to sync recordings with nearby cast members through Apple's local peer connectivity.

Curtain Call does not receive those files or recordings.

Microphone And Speech Recognition

Curtain Call asks for microphone access so users can record scripts and actor line readings.

Curtain Call asks for speech recognition access so users can dictate script text into editable dialogue lines.

Local Network And Bluetooth

Curtain Call asks for local network and Bluetooth-related permissions to find nearby cast workrooms and sync rehearsal recordings between nearby devices.

Purchases

Curtain Call Plus subscriptions are handled by Apple through the App Store. Apple may process purchase, billing, and subscription information under Apple's own terms and privacy policy.

iCloud

The current MVP does not enable iCloud archive or remote sync. Future Pro features may use the user's own iCloud account for storage and sync.

Contact

For support or privacy questions, contact curtaincallsupport@icloud.com.
