Cloud features are subject to per-account request, concurrency, and usage allowances and provider availability. Cloud features may pause when either limit is reached or the service is unavailable. This allowance is not a cash credit or an additional bill, and unused allowance does not carry forward. Pro and existing Plus subscribers retain on-device processing when cloud processing is unavailable. Device models require compatible hardware, software, language, and downloaded model availability.
