For Beta Testers

Please include your iPhone model, iOS version, what you were trying to do, what happened, and whether the issue repeats. Screenshots or screen recordings are helpful when available.

Critical Flows To Report

  • Recording actor lines
  • Playing recorded lines during rehearsal
  • Importing or exporting .cueroomshow files
  • Nearby Workroom sync between two iPhones
  • Microphone, speech recognition, local network, or Bluetooth permission issues
