Cue Room Support

Contact curtaincallsupport@icloud.com for help with Cue Room. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version and a short description of what happened. Don't send scripts you don't have permission to share.

Getting started

Cue Room 2.0 needs iOS 26 or later and works without an account. On first launch, tap Try a short sample scene to rehearse a short sample, or bring in your own script.

Bringing in a script

Tap Import a Script to choose a PDF, Word, RTF or text file, or a Final Draft or Fountain screenplay. You can also use Scan Pages to photograph printed pages, Choose Photos, or Paste Text. Cue Room reads everything on your iPhone, finds the characters and lines, and on supported devices may use Apple Intelligence to check a few unclear passages. Nothing you import is uploaded. Review the cast and lines, fix any names, then choose your part. For unclear scans, use brighter, sharper pages.

Rehearsing

Open a script and tap Rehearse. AI voices read the other parts once you turn them on; until then, and whenever AI voices can't, Apple voices read. When it's your line, say it and pause, or tap Next; Cue Room listens with Apple's speech recognition, on your device when supported. The options menu lets you hide your lines, change reply timing and more. Cue Room remembers where you stopped. You can record your own takes of any line and share a script, with its recordings, with castmates.

Free and Pro

The free version includes one script of your own (the sample doesn't count) with every rehearsal feature and a one-time AI voice preview. Cue Room Pro, a monthly auto-renewable subscription, adds unlimited scripts and AI voices for every line. You don't need to sign in to subscribe. Existing Cue Room Plus subscriptions remain restorable for unlimited scripts. Restore Purchases is in Settings and on the Pro offer.

AI voices

AI voices are the default for every script. To turn them on, tap Turn On AI Voices in a rehearsal, or open AI Voices in Settings: sign in with Apple, allow Cue Room to send the words of voiced lines, and wait for Free preview ready or Pro verified. To change a character's voice, open a script, tap the options button and choose Characters & Voices. The audio is generated by Cue Room's service using Google Cloud Text-to-Speech and saved on your iPhone for reuse. Without Pro you get a one-time free preview, about one scene of lines; Pro includes a fair-use allowance each billing period. If you reach a limit, or the service is unavailable, Apple voices read the remaining lines. The service may take up to a minute to answer after being idle.

Subscription and billing

Apple's purchase sheet shows your localized price before purchase. Use Restore Purchases with the Apple Account that subscribed. Manage or cancel your subscription in Settings in Cue Room or in Apple subscription settings. Deleting the app or your Cue Room account does not cancel billing. For a refund request, visit Apple Report a Problem.

Your data

Scripts, recordings and rehearsal progress stay on your iPhone. Share a script from its menu to keep a copy or move to another device. In Settings you can clear saved AI audio, sign out, or delete your Cue Room account. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details.

Earlier versions

Version 1.2's cloud AI features (OpenAI script analysis, voices and take transcription) have been retired. Update to the current version for AI voices; your scripts and recordings carry over. Devices that can't run iOS 26 can keep using version 1.2's on-device tools.
