Free and Pro

Cue Room is free to download and use without an account. The free version includes one imported script (plus the sample scene) with every rehearsal feature: script import and editing, hands-free listening, recorded takes, sharing, Apple voices and a one-time AI voice preview.

Cue Room Pro is a monthly auto-renewing subscription that adds unlimited scripts and AI voices for every line. Existing Cue Room Plus subscriptions remain restorable and include unlimited scripts. Signing in isn't required to subscribe; signing in with Apple links Pro to your Apple ID so AI voices can be verified across your devices.

The localized price shown on the App Store purchase sheet, including applicable taxes as presented by Apple, is the price you authorize. Payment is charged to your Apple Account on confirmation. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the current period ends; renewal charges may occur within the 24 hours before renewal. No free trial is included unless Apple explicitly presents one.

Manage or cancel in your Apple Account's subscription settings or from Settings in Cue Room. Cancellation takes effect at the end of the paid period. Deleting the app does not cancel billing. Restore purchases with the same Apple Account. Refund requests are handled by Apple at Report a Problem (https://reportaproblem.apple.com). Your saved scripts are never deleted when a subscription ends; you keep using every script already in your library.

AI voices

AI voices are generated by Cue Room's service using Google Cloud Text-to-Speech and are synthetic; they do not imitate real performers. They require Sign in with Apple and your permission to send line text for speech generation. Without Pro, a one-time free preview of about one scene of lines is included; Pro includes AI voices for every line, verified by the service. AI voices are subject to fair-use limits and service availability, and the free preview may be paused when demand is high; when AI voices are unavailable, Apple voices read the lines instead. Generated audio is saved on your device and reused.

Script import, text recognition and the optional Apple Intelligence check run on your device. Automatic reading can misread pages or misidentify speakers; review your script before rehearsing. Speech recognition may miss a cue; Next is always available.
